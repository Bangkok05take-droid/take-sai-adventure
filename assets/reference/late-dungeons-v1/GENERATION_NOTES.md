## Sealed
Use case: stylized-concept. Produce a production 2D fantasy dungeon tileset atlas, square 1024x1024, exactly FOUR columns and FOUR rows of contiguous 256x256 cells, no gutters no labels no text. Each tile fills its cell. Painterly detailed stone art for top-down JRPG, clean readable forms at 32px. Orthographic (not isometric). Rows: row1: four IDENTICAL copies of one neutral walkable stone floor texture, modest tiny cracks, uniform brightness and stone scale, no decorative borders, no symbols, no carpet, no dramatic shading, matching opposite edges. row2: four identical copies of one darker seamless stone wall TOP viewed directly overhead, no perspective. row3: four single-cell straight-on vertical wall FRONT modules, horizontal top and bottom boundaries aligned across row: plain stone panel; plain panel with shallow narrow pilaster; plain stone panel; plain panel with small engraved ornament. No doors, no openings, no floor, no columns protruding outside walls, no torch glow. row4: four overhead wall rim tiles of the same dark wall-top material, light narrow stone coping strip respectively along LEFT edge, RIGHT edge, TOP edge, BOTTOM edge. Strip width exactly24px of256. Material and scale must match row2. These are rectangular usable tiles, not a presentation or scene. No external scenery, abyss, backdrop, floating platforms, perspective corners, huge decorations or UI. All source cells intended downscale to128x128 (then32x32 ingame). Theme: Sealed depths, ancient dark violet-grey masonry, restrained desaturated violet accents on wall ornament only. Walkable floors medium grey lavender with subtle age, walls darker purple charcoal. No luminous floors; purple magical aesthetic suggested only by the tiny wall engraving.
